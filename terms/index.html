What this app is

This app is an independent chat client for Minecraft servers. It is not an official Minecraft product and is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft. Servers are run by other people; we do not run, control or moderate them.

Community rules

Do not post content that is hateful, harassing, threatening, sexually explicit, or that shares other people’s personal information. Do not spam, scam or impersonate others. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Also follow the rules of each server and the game’s own terms.

Blocking and reporting

Long-press a message to block its sender (their messages are hidden on this device) or to report it. A report opens an e-mail to the developer with the message attached. We review every report and act on it as quickly as we can, aiming for 24 hours, including passing it on to the server operators.

Your data

Your server list, accounts, settings and chat history are stored on this device and are not sent to us. On iPhone they can be part of your own iCloud or computer backup of the device. Microsoft sign-in tokens are kept in the device’s secure storage and are only sent to Microsoft and the Minecraft services. Messages you write are sent to the server you are connected to.

No warranty

The app is provided as is. Servers may change, disconnect you or refuse connections at any time. You are responsible for what you send with this app.
